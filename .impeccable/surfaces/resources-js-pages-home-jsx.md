## Scope

Public portfolio (Home, Work index, Case study, About, Contact) plus the admin area (Projects list and editor, Inbox, Login). Mode: Experience for public pages (the work leads), Operate for admin.

Audience: prospective clients and recruiters, equally. Job: understand what Adrian builds, read one case study, start a conversation. One contact action sitewide, labelled "Start a conversation".

Design read: developer portfolio for clients and recruiters, in the Ralph de Vinca perfumery language made minimal. Dials ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 (taste dials VARIANCE 5 / MOTION 4 / DENSITY 3). Light only, because the Ralph de Vinca world is a light paper world; dark mode is a later option, not a default.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every project reads like a fragrance pyramid: Surface (what people see), Function (what it does), Foundation (what holds it up). The page refuses the split hero and the three-equal-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white ground #FAFAF7, ink #1A1A18, Ralph de Vinca gold #A8834F for display words and marks only, darker gold #7A5E33 for small gold text, hairline #E6E2DA, muted #67625A. Display serif Gloock (heavy, high contrast, echoes the Ralph de Vinca headline), body and UI Hanken Grotesk. Pill buttons, square images with 2px radius, hairline rules, no cards, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor learns in one line who Adrian is, sees the strongest project at full width, reads it in three layers, browses the rest, and starts a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav 72px (wordmark left; Work, About, and the pill "Start a conversation" right). Left: Gloock headline up to 76px, two lines, gold "the surface."; 19-word subline; the primary pill with "Open for projects in 2026" beside it. Right, 440px: the Selected work index (project titles in Gloock, category and year, ink rule and "Shown below" on the featured row, no numerals). Below, the featured project plate spans the content width from about y=552 at 1440. Primary action is the pill; the plate and index link to case studies.

FORM: Piramida Aroma (perfume note pyramid), position 4 of 7 on the grounded list, dealt 3rd; seed key a6547049. Signature interaction: the three layer tabs swap the large image to that layer's screenshot (crossfade with 2px blur, 220ms), keyboard operable.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
